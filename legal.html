Terms of participation

This is a charity silent auction run by the organising team of this event (“we”, “us”, “the organisers”). Bids support the cause shown on the The Cause page: Divyatarang Educare Foundation. By placing a bid you agree to these terms.

1. Who can bid

You must be 18 or older, and able to make a binding commitment to pay. Children may not bid. If you give details about a child (name, class and section), you confirm that you are that child's parent or legal guardian, or are authorised by them.

2. Your bid is a promise

  • A bid is a binding offer to buy the item at that price. The highest valid bid when a lot closes wins, once the organisers confirm it.
  • Each bid must beat the current highest bid by at least the minimum increase shown on the item.
  • Large bids are held. Our team will call you on the number you gave to confirm the bid. A held bid does not count as the highest bid until it is confirmed.
  • Unusually large bids are not accepted, and bidding is paused on that account and phone for a few minutes.
  • You can undo your latest bid within 1 minute of placing it, but not in the last 10 minutes before a lot closes. After that, a bid cannot be withdrawn.
  • The time shown by our servers decides whether a bid was placed before a lot closed.

3. Honest details

You must give a real name, a working phone number and a real email address that belong to you. We do not verify the phone number with a code, so we rely on you to enter it correctly. Bids with false or someone else's details may be cancelled.

4. What we may do

To keep the auction fair, the organisers may, at their discretion: reject, hold, revoke or cancel any bid (for example a fake, abusive, mistaken or unverifiable bid); block an account or phone from bidding; correct, close, extend or withdraw a lot; and choose the winner of a lot. The organisers' decision on these matters is final, subject to your rights under law.

5. Payment and collection

This website does not take payment. After a lot closes, the organisers will contact the winner on the phone number given to arrange payment and collection. If a winner cannot be reached or does not pay within the time the organisers set, the organisers may treat the win as void and offer the item to another bidder. We do not promise any tax receipt or tax benefit unless the organisers confirm it to you in writing.

6. The items

Items are described and photographed in good faith. Many are handmade, so colours, sizes and finish can differ slightly from the photos. Items are sold as shown and are not returnable or refundable, except where the law says otherwise.

7. Fair use of the site

Do not use bots or scripts to bid, bid to disturb the auction, try to get around the limits on this site, access other people's data, or enter offensive names or text. Doing so may lead to your bids being removed, your account being blocked and, where needed, being reported to the school or the authorities.

8. Availability and liability

We try to keep the site running, but we cannot promise it will always be available or error-free (for example, a slow network may stop a bid from going through). Check My Bids to confirm a bid was recorded. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for losses caused by outages, network problems or events outside our control. Nothing in these terms limits any liability that cannot be limited by law.

9. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Disputes will be handled by the courts that have jurisdiction where the organisers are based.

10. Changes

We may update these terms and the notices below. The date at the top shows the latest version. Bids already placed stay under the terms that applied when you placed them.

  • To record and run your bids, and to tell which family placed which bid.

  • Google Firebase Hosting serves the website files and may keep ordinary server logs. It may also keep temporary copies of the site's public files (such as images) on servers near you so they load faster. These files contain no personal details.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only for as long as we need them to run the auction, contact winners, collect payment and answer questions about it. After that, the organisers delete or anonymise them.

Your rights and how to reach us

You can ask us to show you the details we hold about you, correct them, erase them, or withdraw your consent. If you withdraw consent or ask for erasure while a bid is still live, we may have to cancel that bid. To ask, or to raise a concern, contact the organisers at the event help desk. If we do not resolve a complaint, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India as the Act provides.

Security

The site uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Database rules limit who can read or write each record, and access to the organiser pages is limited to approved organisers. No online service is perfectly secure, so please do not enter details you are not comfortable sharing.

Cached copies of site files

To make the site faster, your browser keeps temporary copies of the site's files, such as the logo and photos, fonts, page files and scripts. This is a normal browser feature (the “cache”) and it is separate from cookies. Photos and logos are kept for up to one day. Page files and scripts are checked for updates each time you visit, so you always get the latest version.

These cached files are the same public files every visitor receives. They contain no personal details about you, and we do not use them to recognise or track you. Copies of these public files may also be held for speed by Google's servers that host the site and by Google Fonts. Your bids and personal details are not cached this way: they come live from our database. You can clear cached files at any time in your browser's settings (usually “Clear browsing data” → “Cached images and files”). The site still works if you do, just a little slower on the next visit.

Cookies from other companies

If you tap “Sign in with Google”, Google's sign-in service may set its own cookies on Google's domains. We do not control them; see Google's privacy policy. Google (for fonts, site code and hosting) may also receive standard technical data (such as your IP address) when pages load.

Controlling storage

You can clear this data any time in your browser's site settings. Signing out (Profile tab) removes your sign-in and saved email. Clearing storage resets saved form details on that phone; if you sign in again, your bids are reloaded from the server.

We only use storage that the site needs to work, so we do not show a consent pop-up for it. We show a short notice on your first visit so you know it is there.
