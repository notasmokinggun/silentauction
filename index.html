Here's how it works

Every bid supports a good cause. This takes one minute to read. You can open it again any time from the Profile tab.

HOW TO BID
1
Choose an item Tap an item to see its photo and details.
2
Sign in Use your Google account. If you don't have one, just type your email address. You do not need a password.
3
Add your details We need your phone number, your child's name, and either their class and section or their admission number. This tells us which family placed the bid.
4
Place your bid Type the amount you want to bid and tap Confirm Bid. If your bid is above ₹20,000, our team will call you to confirm it first. Made a mistake? You can undo your bid within 1 minute.
THE BASICS
₹
Highest bid Each item shows the highest bid so far. It updates by itself, so you don't need to refresh.
+
Minimum raise Each item shows how much higher your bid must be. Your bid must be at least that much more than the highest bid.
★
Where to see the winners When bidding ends, the winner of each item is shown on the Results tab of this website.
!
Please bid carefully Bids of ₹1,00,000 or more are not accepted. If you try one, bidding is paused for a few minutes on your account and on this phone. Using a different email on the same phone will not skip the pause.
